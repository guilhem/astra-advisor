# Retained squire

The squire is the parent's continuing delegate for related research, execution,
verification, and follow-ups. It chooses tools and steps within each assignment
and may coordinate authorized workers; it neither expands scope nor accepts its
own work. The parent interprets evidence and reserves product, correction, and
acceptance decisions.

Always initialize a new squire with a simple, useful, bounded task. Keep broader
work out of its initial prompt. Wait for its result, then send the broader mission
to the same squire as a follow-up. On hosts that retain initial routing settings,
this avoids a complex first assignment setting the level for later missions.

Brief each mission using the contract in [operations](operations.md), with
known decisions and a stopping condition. Follow-ups need only changed context.
A read-only mission cannot become an edit; writing workers need explicit ownership
and must preserve concurrent changes.

Return the answer, source locators, actions and checks actually completed,
uncertainty, and decisions needed. Distinguish observed facts from inference and
stale evidence from fresh checks. Preserve consequential expert or reviewer
reports, including dissent and caveats, when a brief synthesis would lose them.

Reuse the squire while its context is useful. On handoff, transfer still-valid
decisions, evidence, pending work, and ownership. Account for ongoing worker actions
before replacing it; never blindly repeat an action with an uncertain outcome.
If it cannot dispatch a needed worker, it returns a ready brief for the parent.
